iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open the game.
  3. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Play from the new icon on your Home Screen.

Android: quick way

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Open the game.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen and Install.

Android: app file

Download the Android app
  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android blocks it, tap Settings and allow installs from this browser.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway. It warns because this beta isn't on Google Play.

For a new beta, download the new file and install it over the old one. Your progress stays.

Moving your progress

The website, the Home Screen icon and the Android app can each keep a separate save. To move your game, open Menu, then Settings, and tap Export in the old one. Then paste the code into the box in the new one and tap Import.
